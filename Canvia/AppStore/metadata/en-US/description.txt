Canvia is a complete design studio in your pocket. Make posters, flyers, invitations, presentations, résumés, menus, social posts and stories, then share them anywhere, without an account and without your work ever leaving your device.

START FROM A TEMPLATE
• Over 1,000 templates for social media, events, business, education, food and travel
• Every common size: stories, posts, thumbnails, A4 and Letter, slides, cards and more
• Resize a design for another format in one step

MAKE IT YOURS
• Text with real typography: dozens of font families, weights, spacing, lists, curved and warped text, and effects like shadow, outline, neon and 3D
• Shapes, lines, connectors, icons, stickers and a library of graphics
• Photos with filters, adjustments, crop, background blur and photo frames and grids
• Brand kits that keep your colours, fonts and logos in one place

PRESENT AND ANIMATE
• Build slide decks with charts, tables and diagrams
• Animate words and elements, add transitions, music and voice-over
• Present from your phone, with presenter notes on an external display

SHARE ANYWHERE
• Export as PNG, JPG, PDF, SVG, GIF, video, PowerPoint, Word or a web page
• Print-ready PDFs with bleed and crop marks
• Open PowerPoint, Word, Excel and PDF files and keep editing

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Canvia needs no account and collects no data. There are no ads and no tracking. Your designs, photos and fonts are stored only on your device, and you choose what to share.

ACCESSIBLE
Full VoiceOver support, Dynamic Type, an accessibility checker for your designs and colour-blindness previews.

Canvia is available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese and Arabic.
