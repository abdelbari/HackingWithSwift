# Canvia privacy policy

*Last updated: 6 November 2026*

Canvia is a design app for iPhone, iPad and Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: **Canvia does not collect, store or share any of your personal data.**

## What Canvia keeps, and where

Your designs, the photos, videos and fonts you add, your brand kits and your settings are saved **only on your device**, inside the app's own storage. We never receive copies of them. If you turn on "Back up automatically", the backup is written to a folder **you** choose, such as one in iCloud Drive or on your device, and nowhere else.

## What Canvia does not do

- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It makes no network requests of its own, so nothing you make is uploaded by the app.
- It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs.
- It does not sell or share any data, because it has none to share.

## Permissions

Canvia asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings.

| Permission | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Camera | Taking photos and videos for a design, and scanning documents. |
| Microphone | Dictating text, recording voice-overs, and live captions while presenting. |
| Speech recognition | Turning what you say into text, captions or live captions. |
| Photos (add only) | Saving an exported design to your photo library when you choose "Save to photos". |
| Notifications | Telling you when an export that carried on in the background is ready, and reminders for posts you plan. Both are scheduled on your device. |

### Speech recognition

Canvia asks Apple's speech recogniser to work on your device. On devices or languages where on-device recognition is not available, Apple may process the audio on its servers, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Canvia itself never receives or keeps the audio or the text.

## Sharing

When you share or export a design, you choose where it goes, for example Messages, Mail, Files or another app. What happens next is up to you and the app or service you sent it to.

## Children

Canvia does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
