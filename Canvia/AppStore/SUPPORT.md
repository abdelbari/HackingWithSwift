## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Canvia works without signing in, and everything you make is stored on your device.

**How do I export or share a design?**
Open the design, tap Share and choose Export. Pick a file type (PNG, JPG, PDF, SVG, GIF, video, PowerPoint, Word or web page), then save it to your device or photos, or send it to another app.

**How do I keep my designs safe if I change phones?**
In Settings, turn on "Back up automatically" and choose a folder, for example in iCloud Drive. You can also tap "Back up now", and use "Restore from backup" on your new device.

**Can I open PowerPoint, Word, Excel or PDF files?**
Yes. Open the file from Files, Mail or another app and choose Canvia, or use Import on the Home screen.

**How do I print with bleed and crop marks?**
Tap Share, choose Export, pick "Print-ready PDF" as the file type and turn on Bleed and Crop marks.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. Canvia collects no data and has no ads or tracking. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
